Synopsis

“A desire to subvert things and words, to invent another language”

Over all these years, more than ten, the book and my perception of it have changed, but I have felt the need to stay true to the impulse that transformed me and to the form I found to represent it. Then and now, I find in it a desire to subvert things and words, to invent another language, to build another way of seeing and of listening, and to see what happens when you do.

Óscar Curieses (Madrid, 2022)

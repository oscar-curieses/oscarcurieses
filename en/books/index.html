Translation2026Casus Belli

La poesía como arte insurgente

Lawrence Ferlinghetti · Translated by Óscar Curieses

A collection of Lawrence Ferlinghetti’s most lucid and combative aphoristic writings, one of the essential voices of 20th-century poetry and critical culture: fragments that stand on their own and are, at the same time, the verses of one great poem about poetry, freedom and contemporary society.

Fiction2026El escorpión azul

El operario y la sal

A Chinese tale

A Chinese tale in a handmade cardboard edition: an artisanal book, iconoclastic and combative in just the right measure. The author’s royalties from this title go to aid for Gaza.

Fiction2024Espasa

Tengo la impresión de que el cielo se prepara para la lluvia

With drawings by El Roto and Rábago

The radical risk of giving narrative form to the urgencies of a future as longed for as it is feared. Short, poetic and unsettling stories set in Dirmad —an anagram of Madrid—, a city-state that stands for many other capitals of the world, accompanied by drawings by Andrés Rábago.

Edition and catalogue2023Reservoir Books

Los tiempos bárbaros

OPS · Edited by Óscar Curieses

The final volume of the complete works of OPS, the most untamed alter ego of Andrés Rábago (El Roto). It gathers the drawings he published in La Codorniz, Hermano Lobo and Cuadernos para el Diálogo in the last years of Franco’s regime, whose originals were lost for some forty years until they turned up in an attic.

Translation2023Ya lo dijo Casimiro Parker

Óvulos en la mano

Sharon Olds · Translation and foreword by Óscar Curieses

An anthology by the American poet Sharon Olds with her own commentary, reflecting on some of her poems and on her writing. A way into an incisive, subversive and committed poetics, in a bilingual edition.

Poetry2022Varasek Ediciones

Libro de los icebergs

“Over all these years, more than ten, the book and my perception of it have changed, but I have felt the need to stay true to the impulse that transformed me and to the form I found to represent it. Then and now, I find in it a desire to subvert things and words, to invent another language, to build another way of seeing and of listening, and to see what happens when you do.”

Essay2022Huerga y Fierro

En el cine de Auster

Foreword by Manuel Gutiérrez Aragón

Why watch Paul Auster’s films, what is in them and how does he conceive them? The only book published on his entire filmography, moving between his literature and his cinema, which for Auster is as fundamental as his books.

Edition and catalogue2022RM

OPS, El Roto, Rábago. Una microhistoria del mundo

Catalogue of the exhibition curated by Óscar Curieses

The work of Andrés Rábago through his three heteronyms —the Dadaist gaze of OPS, the social critique of El Roto, the painting of Rábago—, with a series of micro-stories written by Óscar Curieses for each of the exhibition’s recurring themes.

Poetry2021Ya lo dijo Casimiro Parker

Constitución Española

Text intervention by Óscar Curieses

Are emptying and emptiness the same thing? An intervention on the text of the Spanish Constitution, inspired partly by a 1917 poem by Hemingway and by Spanish politics over the last decade, with essays by Dora García, Francisco Baena, Virginia Trueba Mira and Eduardo Gómez Cuadrado.

Translation2017Bartleby Editores

La célula de oro

Sharon Olds · Translation and foreword by Óscar Curieses

Perhaps the most accessible of Sharon Olds’s books published in Spain: the emotional intensity of The Father and the range of settings of The Dead and the Living, with irony and beauty, free of metaphor and fantasy. Olds won the Pulitzer Prize for Poetry in 2013.

Translation2017Amargord

La comedia eléctrica

Rolando Pérez · Translation and foreword by Óscar Curieses

Taking Dante’s Divine Comedy as its starting point, Rolando Pérez offers a journey through the present day —electronic simulation, digital capitalism, virtual reality—: a poetic, philosophical and humorous response to the infernal conditions of neoliberalism.

Fiction2014Jekyll and Jill

Hombre en azul

The personal diary of the painter Francis Bacon, written by Óscar Curieses: a fiction that traces his career and his thinking and that blurs, as in his paintings, the boundaries between painter and writer. It comes with a pair of 3D glasses.

Poetry2013Ya lo dijo Casimiro Parker

Hay una jaula en cada pájaro

Verbal experimentation must always be bound to the experience of poetry. For Óscar Curieses those two worlds are one, and he brings them together to reconcile reality and life.

Poetry2010Bartleby Editores

Dentro

Óscar Curieses’s second poetry collection: a space prior to life, of infinite possibility and threatened at once by ruin, violence and death, where words work as mirrors. “Everything happens inside: of the flesh, of the imagination, of the word.”

Poetry2007Bartleby Editores

Sonetos del útero

Language as matter and the body as memory and language: a book of sonnets in which matter, like poetry, is womb and abyss, a place of metamorphosis where birth and death, destruction and creation meet.

Spoken word and performance

La poesía,
si es que existe

La poesía, si es que existe (Poetry, if it exists) was born to turn the written word into a multidisciplinary artistic experience. Spoken word, video projections, photographs, live music and improvisation are some of the resources used in this performance, in which the word is always the essential element.

Using Óscar Curieses’s literary work as raw material, his texts are rewritten live and mixed with other disciplines so that the audience can experience them from a new and unexpected perspective, always through an intense, minimalist staging. Each performance offers a renewed version of his work and of all the elements woven into it.

A series of family photographs taken more than half a century ago, a composition by Bob Dylan, ambient music behind a text by John of the Cross, or even a video recording of a train journey made on a mobile phone.

A project by Óscar Curieses · with Pablo Ramírez

Pieces

La poesía, si es que existe #1

Train to Machado

Óscar CuriesesPablo Ramírez12–15 minutes

Train to Machado is a video projection accompanied by a free recitation of the poem Esa fue la voz que escuché. Ponis que se agitan como los árboles en las montañas otoñales, by Óscar Curieses.

The piece was first performed as an audio performance with the group AMC313 and appeared on the CD El grito es un movimiento inacabado, included in the book Hay una jaula en cada pájaro (Ya lo dijo Casimiro Parker, 2013). In it, Antonio Machado, travelling by train, recalls the poems that Raymond Carver and Derek Walcott dedicated to him years after his death, while he gazes at the Castilian landscape.

La poesía, si es que existe #4

W+D. Whitman plus Dylan

Óscar CuriesesPablo Ramírez12 minutes

Whitman plus Dylan is the fourth project of La poesía, si es que existe.

This piece combines Óscar Curieses’s poem La barba de Walt Whitman (Walt Whitman’s Beard) with a variation on Bob Dylan’s Main Title Theme, from the film Pat Garrett and Billy the Kid (1973). The theme is freely reworked and played on guitar and harmonica by Pablo Ramírez.

While lyrics and music are partly improvised, a portrait of the young Walt Whitman turns into the iconic image of the poet in old age, through a very slow fade lasting some twelve minutes.

La poesía, si es que existe #5

Libro de los icebergs

Óscar CuriesesVariable length (10 to 25 min)

In this piece the texts of Libro de los icebergs (Varasek, 2022) are improvised while being projected, playing with and deconstructing the audience’s reception and reading.

All the poems are conceived as a kind of palindrome that can be read in both directions: from top to bottom (left to right) and from bottom to top (right to left). These live textual constructions are in tune with some of the themes running through the book: memory, polar landscapes, love and the political moment in which it was written. A loop of ambient music used as a mantra stitches the whole together.

La poesía, si es que existe #6

Biolencias

Óscar Curieses10 minutes

In Biolencias, a series of seven texts unfolds the author’s family tree through hereditary flaws and their passing from one ancestor to another until their final dissolution.

On screen, seven old photographs of family ancestors follow one another, like the sections of the poem of the same name. In the background, music mixing noise and ambient blends all these elements together.
